How It Works

From WhatsApp message to
delivered print in 6 steps

A seamless workflow that automates your entire print production pipeline.

1

Customer

Customer sends a WhatsApp message with print requirements — file, quantity, size, paper type.

2

WhatsApp

Our system auto-captures the order from WhatsApp, extracts details, and creates a job ticket instantly.

3

AI Analysis

AI analyzes the job — identifies print specs, estimates cost, checks customer history, and flags any issues.

4

Printer Routing

Smart routing engine assigns the job to the optimal printer based on workload, capability, and urgency.

5

Billing

GST invoice is auto-generated, payment collected via UPI or card, and receipt sent to customer on WhatsApp.

6

Delivery

Track production, manage delivery, update status automatically. Customer gets notified at every stage via WhatsApp.
